Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum cover depth required over a driveway culvert pipe?
To withstand heavy delivery trucks and fire engines (AASHTO H-20 loading of 32,000 lbs per axle), flexible corrugated plastic HDPE pipe requires at least **12 inches of compacted crushed gravel cover** above the crown of the pipe. If pipe cover is less than 12 inches, heavy axles will crush or deform the pipe.
Why is dual-wall smooth interior HDPE pipe superior to corrugated metal pipe (CMP)?
Standard corrugated metal galvanized pipes rust out and rot along the invert (waterline) within 15 to 20 years. Modern dual-wall HDPE pipe (like ADS N-12) has a smooth interior wall that increases water flow velocity by 40% while having an expected service life exceeding 75+ years with zero corrosion.
How do you calculate culvert length for a driveway?
A culvert must be longer than the actual driveway width! Because ditch banks slope outward at a 2:1 angle to prevent erosion, add twice the ditch depth on each end. For a 16-foot driveway over a 28-inch deep ditch, you need at least a 24-foot culvert pipe.
